## ◈ BEYOND HUMAN LOGIC

I'm an Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning undergraduate at **Chitkara University**, exploring what happens when computation meets intelligence.

I'm drawn to the process of turning raw information into meaningful decisions, mathematical ideas into algorithms, and challenging questions into possibilities. I want to understand technology beyond the surface — the principles that make intelligent systems work, the choices behind their design, and the opportunities they create.

> **The future isn't just written in code. It's shaped by the questions we ask.**
